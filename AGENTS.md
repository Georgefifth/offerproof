# OfferProof project instructions

## Purpose

Build a working, judge-ready browser prototype that helps students examine internship and part-time job invitations before sending money or identity documents. A user should be able to paste an invitation, see exact evidence for warning signs, complete an independent employer check, and export a review record.

## Product rules

- Never claim that an offer is certainly genuine or fraudulent from message text alone. Use "warning signs found", "independently confirmed", and "still unverified".
- Show the exact passage that triggered each warning and explain the corresponding action.
- Treat links, addresses, and phone numbers inside the invitation as untrusted. Tell users to find the employer's contact details independently.
- Keep the invitation in the browser. Do not send it to a server, analytics provider, or AI API. Avoid persistent storage of invitation content.
- Do not require an account, API key, payment, or remote service to complete the core flow.
- Make the English interface usable by judges and provide Chinese text where practical.

## Competition and honesty

- LovHack Season 3 build period: September 26 through October 4, 2026, Eastern time. Submission deadline: October 4, 2026 at 11:45 pm EDT.
- Explain what was built during the event. Disclose any prior or third-party components and cite sources. Do not invent validation, accuracy statistics, real users, or completed integrations.
- Prioritize a working demo, a 2–3 minute video script, and an accessible submission description. Do not submit or publish without the user's explicit direction.
